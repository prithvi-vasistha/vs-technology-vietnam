How machine vision works

From light to inspection.

Follow one machined part through a vision system to see where VS Technology’s lighting, lenses, and imaging solutions fit.

01Illuminate

Make the detail visible.

Lighting determines what the camera can see. A ring light illuminates the area around a part, while a backlight can reveal its outline. VS Technology offers ring, bar, coaxial, and backlights for different inspection tasks.

Explore industrial lighting

02Capture

Bring the part into focus.

The lens forms an image on the camera sensor. The right lens depends on the size of the part, the available working distance, and the detail you need. VST’s range includes telecentric, fixed focal length, macro, and line-scan lenses.

Explore machine vision lenses

03Inspect

Use the image to check the part.

Image processing can find edges, check dimensions, or locate an alignment mark. VS Technology helps teams choose the optics and lighting for the features their system needs to inspect.

Find the right setup for your application

Start with what your system needs to see, then choose the optics and lighting together.

Telecentric lenses help limit perspective error when measuring the size or position of a part. Backlighting can make the outline easier to detect.

Choose lighting that brings out the target detail. Diffuse illumination can reduce glare on reflective parts, while directional lighting can help reveal scratches.

Use controlled contrast to make boundaries and alignment marks easier to locate before assembly, positioning or inspection.
